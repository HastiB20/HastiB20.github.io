Research 01

I’m a public health researcher, with a background in mathematics and machine learning. I enjoy working with data, making sense of its structure, asking what it can tell us, and exploring it.

Public health, people, and environments

Social and environmental epidemiology examines how the conditions in which people live shape patterns of health across populations. I’m drawn to this perspective and to population mental health: questions that connect individual experiences with the wider world.

Cognition and computational approaches

Computational neuroscience and cognitive modeling use mathematical and computational frameworks to explore the brain and the processes behind cognition. Alongside cognitive development, these are fields I’m excited to explore, bringing together my interests in mathematics, learning, and human health.

Causal Inference

An association can reveal a pattern without explaining what would happen if we changed something. Causal inference addresses that distinction, using explicit assumptions and careful study design to investigate the effects of exposures and interventions. In health research, this means thinking closely about confounding, the timing of measurements, and whether the available data can support the question being asked.

AI in Health

AI can help identify patterns in complex health data, but a useful model needs to do more than perform well on a single dataset. Its value also depends on how it handles uncertainty, how reliably it works across populations, and whether its outputs help answer a meaningful clinical or public health question. Careful validation, attention to bias, and a clear account of a model’s limitations are central to that work.

Methods, evidence, and reproducibility

My experience includes meta-learning, causal inference, longitudinal cohort analysis, and systematic reviews. I’m interested in clinical data analysis and reproducible computational methods for biomedical research, with an appreciation for clean data and analyses that others can understand, inspect, and build on.
