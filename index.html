Hello, I’m Hasti.

I'm a Public health researcher with a mathematician's brain and a machine learner's curiosity. I LOVE data, and it loves me back when it is clean! I have expereince in meta-learning, causal inference, longitudinal cohort analysis, and systematic reviews, and I'm always excited to learn something new. What dives me is the opportunity to work in social and environmental epidemiology, population mental health, cognitive development, computational neuroscience and cognitive modeling, clinical data analysis, and reproducible computational methods for biomedical research.

Outside of work, I write (sometimes poetry; I've published a book and I used to attend poetry nights), I walk in the woods, and meditate. I enjoy to be alone sometimes, watch movies, and THINK. I love cinema, I love fashion styling, and I love narrating. I eat a lot haha! And I am in love with chocolate, however, I'm trying to eat less becasue of suger. (We will have fun if you vibe!)
